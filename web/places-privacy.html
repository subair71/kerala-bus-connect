Place search privacy

When Google place suggestions are enabled and you type at least three characters in a place field, FindMyBus sends that query through its Firebase backend to Google Places. App Check verifies requests. Your device location is not requested for this feature.

Suggestions are used while you search and are not saved as a Google places catalogue. Selecting a suggestion fills an editable field. Owners save their confirmed route and stop names through the existing save actions.

Google processes these requests under the Google Privacy Policy. See also Place search terms.
